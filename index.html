Frequently Asked Questions

Instant answers on design, fabrication, and payments.

Can I design in exact real-world dimensions (feet, mm, inches)?

Yes. The studio lets you switch between feet, inches, millimeters, centimeters, and meters. Your exports maintain 300 DPI print-ready scaling.

What formats can I export?

You can export PNG, JPG, WEBP, vector SVG, 300 DPI PDF, and CorelDRAW v12 compatible prepress files.

How does the 50% advance payment work?

Commercial signage fabrication begins once the 50% advance is verified via UPI (ledsignadil@oksbi) or PayPal. The remaining 50% is cleared upon quality check before dispatch.

Can I upload my existing logo or reference file?

Yes. Upload PNG, JPG, or SVG in the Logo panel. Our AI can automatically place it at the optimal position.
